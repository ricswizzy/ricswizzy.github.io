IHK Exam Preparation

How the IHK Fachinformatiker Exam Works

The Fachinformatiker Abschlussprüfung (final exam) uses a Gestreckte Abschlussprüfung (GAP) format split into two parts. AP1 takes place roughly in the middle of the training (after ~18 months) and AP2 at the end. Both parts contribute to your final grade. Most international students underestimate AP1 — it counts for 20% of the overall result and many are unprepared. Eric helps you prepare for both.

AP1 — Teil 1

Einrichten eines IT-gestützten Arbeitsplatzes

90 minutes · 20% of final grade · Written exam
  • Setting up and configuring a workplace IT system
  • Hardware components and their function
  • Software installation and configuration
  • Network connection and basic troubleshooting
  • Data protection and IT security basics
  • Customer communication and documentation
  • English language questions integrated throughout
AP2

Planen & Administrieren von IT-Systemen

240 minutes total · 80% of final grade
  • Systemintegration (90 min): Network planning, server administration, virtualisation
  • Wirtschaft & Sozialkunde (60 min): Business law, labour law, social insurance
  • Projektdokumentation: Written project report (submitted online to IHK)
  • Präsentation & Fachgespräch (30 min): Present and defend your project
