Why Learn German With Eric?

Most German teachers learned German as their native language. Eric learned it as an outsider — coming from Nigeria, navigating the same confusion, making the same mistakes, and figuring out what actually works for English-speaking Africans learning German from scratch.

That experience makes a real difference. Eric knows which parts of German are hardest for English speakers, which grammar rules trip up Nigerians specifically, and how to explain things in a way that makes sense without a textbook full of German instructions you can't yet read.

Every lesson is taught in English with German introduced gradually — so you're never lost wondering what the teacher just said. The focus is always practical: what do you need for your visa? For your workplace? For your IHK exam? That's what gets prioritised.

What You Learn — By Level

The curriculum follows the official CEFR framework (A1 → A2 → B1) and is aligned with the Goethe-Zertifikat exam requirements. Click each level to see exactly what is covered.

A1 — Absolute Beginner

At A1 you'll be able to introduce yourself, handle basic everyday interactions, understand simple instructions, and communicate your most essential needs. This is where everyone starts — zero German knowledge required.

~3 months · 5hrs/week 8 lessons per module Goethe A1 exam aligned

Speaking & Introductions

  • Greetings and farewells (formal & informal)
  • Introducing yourself: name, age, country, occupation
  • Asking and answering basic questions
  • du vs. Sie — when to use formal and informal
  • German alphabet and pronunciation rules
  • Special characters: ä, ö, ü, ß

Numbers, Time & Dates

  • Numbers 1–1,000 and beyond
  • Telling time (Wie spät ist es?)
  • Days of the week and months
  • Dates and birthdays
  • Prices and money (shopping vocabulary)
  • Ordinal numbers (first, second, third…)

Everyday Vocabulary

  • Family members and relationships
  • Food and drink — ordering in a restaurant/café
  • Home and furniture vocabulary
  • Clothing and colours
  • Hobbies and free time activities
  • Common places in a city (Bahnhof, Supermarkt…)

Grammar Foundations

  • German noun genders (der, die, das) — with memory tricks
  • Definite and indefinite articles
  • Verb conjugation: sein, haben, and regular verbs
  • Nominative and accusative cases (basic)
  • Negation: nicht and kein/keine
  • Basic sentence structure and word order

Practical Situations

  • Filling in registration forms (Anmeldeformular)
  • Asking for directions and understanding answers
  • Shopping and paying (buying everyday items)
  • Making simple requests and apologies
  • Using public transport vocabulary
  • Understanding simple written signs and notices

Listening & Reading

  • Understanding slow, clear spoken German
  • Recognising key words in announcements
  • Reading simple texts and short messages
  • Understanding simple advertisements and menus
  • Practice with official Goethe A1 listening exercises
  • Reading comprehension: forms, cards, short letters

A2 — Elementary

At A2 you can communicate in familiar everyday situations, talk about your past and future, handle shopping, appointments and travel, and start understanding more complex texts. A2 is the bridge between beginner and independent use.

~3 months · 5hrs/week 8 lessons per module Goethe A2 exam aligned

Past Tense (Perfekt & Präteritum)

  • Perfekt tense — talking about things you did
  • Regular and irregular past participles
  • haben vs. sein as auxiliary verbs
  • Präteritum of sein and haben (written form)
  • Talking about your weekend, your day, your past
  • Time expressions: gestern, letzte Woche, vor drei Tagen

Work & Professional Life

  • Job titles and workplace vocabulary
  • Describing your job and daily work routine
  • Making appointments by phone and email
  • Simple professional communication
  • Understanding job advertisements
  • Workplace phrases and small talk

Travel & Transport

  • Booking tickets and accommodation
  • Understanding train and bus timetables
  • Describing a journey or holiday
  • At the hotel and at the airport
  • Asking and giving detailed directions
  • Travel vocabulary: Koffer, Flug, Verspätung…

Grammar — A2 Level

  • Dative case — indirect objects and prepositions
  • Dative articles: dem, der, einem, einer
  • Separable verbs (aufmachen, anrufen, etc.)
  • Modal verbs: können, müssen, wollen, dürfen, sollen
  • Comparative and superlative adjectives
  • Subordinate clauses with weil, dass, wenn

Health & Daily Life

  • Body parts and health vocabulary
  • Describing symptoms at the doctor (Arzttermin)
  • Pharmacy vocabulary (Apotheke)
  • Talking about daily routines in detail
  • Housing: finding a flat, reading rental ads
  • Banking basics: opening an account, transactions

Writing & Reading A2

  • Writing short messages and emails
  • Filling in official forms correctly
  • Reading and understanding short newspaper articles
  • Understanding longer announcements and notices
  • Writing a simple formal letter (Anschreiben basics)
  • Goethe A2 practice: writing tasks and reading texts

B1 — Intermediate (Visa Level)

B1 is the minimum German level required for the Ausbildung visa and is the target for most people planning to move to Germany. At B1 you can understand the main points of clear standard German, handle most travel and work situations, and express yourself on familiar topics. The Goethe-Zertifikat B1 is accepted by the German Embassy worldwide.

~4 months · 5hrs/week 10 lessons per module Goethe B1 exam aligned Required for Ausbildung Visa

Expressing Opinions & Discussions

  • Agreeing, disagreeing and giving reasons
  • Discussing advantages and disadvantages
  • Expressing wishes, hopes and plans (Konjunktiv II)
  • Talking about hypothetical situations
  • Formal vs. informal registers in speech
  • Debate vocabulary and linking phrases

German for the Workplace & Ausbildung

  • Job interview preparation in German
  • Understanding and writing a German Lebenslauf
  • Anschreiben (cover letter) structure and phrases
  • Workplace communication: emails, meetings, requests
  • IT and technical German vocabulary (Fachsprache)
  • Understanding instructions and work contracts

Grammar — B1 Level

  • Passive voice (Passiv) — active vs. passive sentences
  • Reflexive verbs (sich freuen, sich vorstellen…)
  • Konjunktiv II — wishes, politeness, hypotheticals
  • Genitive case and two-way prepositions
  • Adjective endings without articles
  • Relative clauses (der/die/das as relative pronouns)

Reading & Listening B1

  • Understanding newspaper articles and online texts
  • Reading longer formal letters and documents
  • Listening to news and podcasts at normal speed
  • Understanding telephone conversations
  • Goethe B1 past paper reading and listening practice
  • Understanding Berufsschule materials and handouts

Writing B1

  • Writing formal and informal letters and emails
  • Describing events, experiences and processes
  • Giving and justifying your opinion in writing
  • Writing a complaint letter or an official request
  • Goethe B1 writing task practice (Schreiben)
  • Writing a simple Lebenslauf and Motivationsschreiben

Speaking B1 — Exam Preparation

  • Describing photos and situations (Bildbeschreibung)
  • Role play: handling official and professional scenarios
  • Presenting a topic and answering follow-up questions
  • Goethe B1 Sprechen exam format and practice
  • Mock speaking exam sessions with feedback
  • Pronunciation and fluency drills
Goethe-Zertifikat Exam Preparation

The Goethe-Zertifikat is the most recognised German language certificate worldwide — accepted by the German Embassy, all German universities, and most employers. Eric prepares you specifically for the exam format across all four skills: reading, writing, listening, and speaking.

Where to Take the Exam in Nigeria

The official Goethe-Institut Nigeria is located at 7 Anifowoshe Street, Victoria Island, Lagos. Exams for A1 and A2 can be booked directly on their website. For B1 and B2 in Nigeria, contact them to confirm current exam dates: goethe.de/ins/ng.
